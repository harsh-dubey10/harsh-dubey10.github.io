About this build

I'm an M.Tech student in Computer Science at IIT Gandhinagar, working with the FUSS Group under Prof. Abhishek Bichhawat. I like building systems that sit at the intersection of AI and real engineering — distributed multi-agent pipelines, NLP tools for underserved languages, and the occasional full-stack app to see an idea through end to end.

Before this, I topped out my B.Tech at Bhilai Institute of Technology with a department rank of 3 out of 125, qualified GATE CS 2025, and spent a summer building data-driven product experiments as an intern. I enjoy the parts of engineering most people skip — the evaluation, the error analysis, the "why did this actually break."
